# Tyler Boyd

Engineer, attorney, and founder. I love finding a problem and owning the whole loop: the idea, the interface, the AI, and the evals that show what works and what still needs work.

I co-founded a medical device and AI company, and I build AI research tools, private writing apps, and the occasional browser game. More at [boydwrites.com/projects](https://www.boydwrites.com/projects).

## Projects

- **[Personal Commentary](https://github.com/boydwrites/personal-commentary)**: Build your own commentary on Scripture, with AI research whose quotations are checked against their sources.
- **[Folio](https://github.com/boydwrites/folio-editor)**: A private AI writing editor that runs entirely on your Mac. Every edit is shown inline and can be undone.
- **[Starwake](https://github.com/boydwrites/starwake)**: Collect every star and survive the swarm in a browser arcade game. [Play it](https://boydwrites.github.io/starwake/).
- **[Petal Chase](https://github.com/boydwrites/petal-chase)**: Catch the golden petals and dodge the pink ones in a small cursor game. [Play it](https://boydwrites.github.io/petal-chase/).
